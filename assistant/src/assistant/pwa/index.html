A few preferences.

Notifications

Notifications include reply and reminder previews. A device already showing the conversation is not notified, and while it is, neither are your other devices. Message content may appear on your lock screen; control previews in your device notification settings.

Install __AGENT_NAME__

Use your browser’s install menu. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. Install before enabling notifications.

Your conversations

Paste, drop or attach up to four images per message; they are stored in the vault like Telegram photos. The microphone button appears when voice transcription is configured on the server and puts the transcript in the text field for you to review.

Telegram and web messages share a private archive on your server. The latest five complete exchanges are restored after restart; older messages can be retrieved when needed. Reset context starts fresh without deleting the archive; a divider in the chat marks where the model stopped seeing earlier messages.

There is no app password. Access is controlled by your private network. Anyone who can reach this service can use the assistant and read its conversations.
